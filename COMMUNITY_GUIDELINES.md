# zappd Community Guidelines

zappd works because drivers tell each other the truth about chargers. Keep it useful and kind.

## Do

- Post what you actually see: whether the charger works, the queue, the speed you got.
- Keep zaps about charging: the screen, the spot, the queue, the food nearby.
- Review the charger and the place, not the people.
- Mark facilities only when you've seen them yourself.

## Don't

- **Show other people's number plates or faces.** Use the privacy blur in the editor.
- Post fake statuses, spam, ads, or anything unrelated to charging.
- Harass, insult or threaten anyone, including staff at charging stations.
- Post sexual, violent, hateful or illegal content.
- Share someone's personal details.

## What happens if someone breaks the rules

- Anyone can report a zap, review, food tip or driver. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically while we review it.
- We remove content and accounts that break these guidelines, and may block repeat offenders permanently.
- You can block any driver to stop seeing their posts.
