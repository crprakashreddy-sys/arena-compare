Community guidelines

zingd works because drivers help each other. Keep it useful and kind.

Do

  • Post real, recent photos of chargers and say honestly whether they work.
  • Add chargers that are open to the public.
  • Share fixes and tips, and mark the answer that solved your issue.
  • Be welcoming to new EV owners.

Don't

  • Harass, threaten, bully or impersonate anyone.
  • Post hate speech, sexual content, violence or anything illegal.
  • Post fake reports, fake chargers, or game the Zing Score (for example with faked locations).
  • Spam, advertise or sell without the club's permission.
  • Share other people's private information, number plates or faces without consent.
  • Post advice that could be dangerous, such as tampering with batteries or chargers.

Reporting and blocking

Long-press or use ⋯ on any post or person to report or block. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically. We review reports and may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules, without notice. Club admins can also remove messages and members in their clubs.

  • Charger information: charger details, status and charging-time estimates come from drivers and public data. They can be wrong or out of date. Always check the charger and its operator's app before relying on it, and plan with a safe battery reserve. zingd does not run any charging network and doesn't handle charging payments.
