# zappd Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

zappd is a community app that helps electric-vehicle drivers in India find working chargers and share what they see. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address (from Apple, Google or the email you enter), your EV model and city if you add them.
- **Vehicle number (optional):** only if you add it for the Verified EV badge. It is kept private, is never shown to other users, and is used only to check that the vehicle is electric and to stop one vehicle being used for many accounts.
- **Location:** with your permission, your device's location while the app is open, to show chargers near you and to suggest the charger you are at when you drop a zap. We do not store your location history. Search requests send the area you are looking at to our map and charger data providers.
- **What you post:** zaps (photos, status and notes), reviews, facility marks, food tips, and connection requests. Zap photos are deleted automatically 24 hours after posting. The status part of a zap is kept to show charger history and count your points.
- **Safety records:** reports you make and people you block.

We do not collect contacts, browsing history or advertising identifiers, and there are no ads or third-party trackers in the app.

## Who can see what

- Your name, EV model, city, Verified EV badge, points, zaps, reviews, food tips and number of connections are visible to other zappd users.
- Who you are connected with is visible only to you and that person.
- Your email, vehicle number, reports and blocks are never shown to other users.

## Services we use

- **Supabase** (database, sign-in and photo storage).
- **Apple Maps / Google Maps** (map display and directions).
- **Open Charge Map** (charger listings).
- **OpenStreetMap / Overpass and Photon** (nearby food and place search).
- **Apple and Google** (sign-in).
- Optionally, a vehicle-registration check provider, if Verified EV checks are switched on.

Each of these services processes only what it needs to provide its part of the app.

## Your choices

- Turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time. The map still works when you search a place.
- Delete any zap you posted from the zap viewer.
- Edit your profile at any time.
- **Delete your account** under You › Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, zaps, photos, reviews, food tips, facility marks and connections.

## Children

zappd is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 18.
