Common questions

A charger is missing or wrong

Open the map, tap "Missing a charger? Add it", or drop a zing at the charger with the right status.

How is Zing Score worked out?

You earn more points the closer you are to the charger when you post, plus a bonus for a photo and for being the first in 3 hours. See Zings → Leaderboard → How Zing Score works.

How do I delete my account?

You tab → Delete account. Everything you posted is removed.

Someone is being abusive

Long-press their message or post → Report, or Block them. Reports from several people hide content automatically.

Can I pay for charging in zingd?

Not yet. On a charger page, tap "Pay in … app" to open that network's own app.
