zingd is an app for electric-vehicle drivers in India to find chargers, share live charger photos ("zings") and talk with other owners. It is run by Prakash, an individual developer in Hyderabad, India ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version
  • The map works without an account.
  • We never sell your data and there are no ads or ad trackers in zingd.
  • Your exact location is never stored or shown. When you post a zing we save only how far you were from the charger.
  • You can delete your account and everything you posted from inside the app, any time.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address, or your Apple / Google sign-in IDWhen you create an accountTo sign you in. Not shown to other users.
Display name, profile photo or cartoon avatar, bio, city, car modelWhen you set up your profileShown to other drivers on your posts, in clubs and on leaderboards.
Vehicle registration number (optional)Only if you ask for the Verified EV badgeChecked once to confirm the vehicle is electric. Kept private and never shown to anyone.
Photos, zings, reviews, facility votes, food tips, issues, replies, chat messages, events and RSVPsWhen you post themTo show them to other drivers. Zings disappear from the app after 24 hours.

Connections, club memberships, blocks and reportsWhen you use those featuresTo run the community and keep it safe.
Your vehicles, battery level, charging log and savingsWhen you add themStored only on your phone.

We do not collect your contacts, health data or browsing history, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who else handles data

  • Supabase stores your account and posts (our database and file storage).
  • Apple and Google, if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Open Charge Map provides public charger data; the app sends it the map area you are looking at.
  • OpenStreetMap services (Photon, Overpass) and OSRM power place search, nearby food and trip routes; the app sends them the places or route you search for.
  • Apple Maps or Google Maps draw the map and open directions when you tap them.
  • Our email provider sends your sign-in codes.

These services only get what they need to do their job. We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

What other people can see

Anything you post (zings, reviews, tips, issues, replies, events) and your public profile (name, photo or avatar, bio, city, car model, badges and Zing Score) can be seen by other zingd users. Club chats and issues are visible to members of that club. Invite-only clubs are visible only to their members. Your email and registration number are never shown.

How long we keep it

  • Zings are hidden from the app after 24 hours.
  • Everything else stays until you delete it or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, posts, photos and messages are deleted from our systems, apart from copies we must keep for a short time for security or by law.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: You tab → Delete account. This removes your profile and everything you posted.
  • Edit or delete posts: long-press or use the ⋯ menu on anything you posted.
  • Location and camera: turn them on or off any time in iPhone Settings → zingd.
  • Access, correction, erasure and grievances: under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to erase it, or to raise a complaint. Email zingd.support@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Safety and moderation

You can report posts and people and block anyone. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically and reviewed. See our Terms & community guidelines.

Age

zingd is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and our database uses access rules so people can only change their own content. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects you.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.
