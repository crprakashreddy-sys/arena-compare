Data sources and assumptions

Ex-showroom prices are national list prices. We checked Swift, Creta and Nexon in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru and Chennai (Autocar India, Oct 2026) and Maruti/rival prices in Hyderabad, Bhubaneswar, Vijayawada and Raipur (CarWale): ex-showroom was identical in every city. On-road price (road tax, insurance) differs by state and is not included.

Fuel prices by city: petrol from Business Today (31 Oct–1 Nov 2026; a few cities from their latest October listing); CNG from Goodreturns state pages and Aaj Tak (Sep–Nov 2026). Srinagar has no city-gas CNG network listed, so CNG is not offered there. You can edit any price in ⚙️ Settings.

Mileage is the manufacturer's claimed figure for every car. Real-world is assumed at 80% of claimed (change in Settings). EVs: efficiency = claimed range ÷ battery size; charging cost assumed ₹9/kWh at home (change in Settings). e Vitara prices are with battery (no BaaS). EV rival prices: Rushlane (Feb 2026), ranges: Rushlane / MeraEV.

Invicto and Innova Hycross: ex-showroom from Autocar India (Mumbai/Navi Mumbai, Oct 2026); claimed 23.24 km/L hybrid, 16.13 km/L Hycross petrol (CarWale). Invicto hybrids are matched with Hycross hybrids.

Jimny: Mahindra Thar and Force Gurkha don't publish claimed mileage, so a like-for-like running-cost comparison isn't shown.

SIP in the shared image assumes 10% a year, for illustration only. Returns are not guaranteed; not investment advice.
